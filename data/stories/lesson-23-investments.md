title_zh: 怎麼投資
---

Green Leaf had some extra money. Mr. Lin wanted to {invest|6} it {wisely|12}, so he met a financial advisor to talk about it.

> 綠葉有一筆多出來的錢。林先生想把它投資得明智一點，所以去找了一位理財顧問談談。

The advisor asked about his {attitude|2} toward risk. "I'm {conservative|4}," Mr. Lin said. "I don't want to invest {aggressively|1}. I want a {long-term|7} plan." So the advisor suggested a safe {fund|5} that buys many different stocks.

> 顧問問他對風險的態度。林先生說：「我很保守，不想投資得太積極，我想要一個長期的計畫。」於是顧問建議了一檔買進許多不同股票的安全基金。

She also built a {portfolio|8} with bonds and some cash. "The {return|11} may be small, but it is steady," she said. "Don't {pull out|9} your money when the market goes down."

> 她也規劃了一個包含債券和一些現金的投資組合。她說：「報酬也許不高，但很穩定。市場下跌時，不要把錢撤出來。」

In the end, Mr. Lin decided to {commit|3} only part of the money. "Our people are our most important {resource|10}," he said. "I will use the rest to train them."

> 最後，林先生決定只投入一部分的錢。他說：「我們的員工才是最重要的資源，剩下的錢我要用來培訓他們。」
