It was tax season, and the {deadline|2} was May 31. Mia had never done her taxes alone before. She almost wanted to {give up|5}, but Ms. Chen offered to help.

> 報稅季到了，截止期限是 5 月 31 日。米雅從來沒有自己報過稅，她差點想放棄，但陳小姐說可以幫她。

"The company {withholds|12} some tax from your pay every month," Ms. Chen explained. "If too much was taken, you will get a {refund|10}. If not, you will {owe|7} money."

> 陳小姐解釋：「公司每個月會從妳的薪水預扣一些稅。如果扣太多，妳會拿到退稅；如果扣不夠，妳就要補繳。」

Together, they {filled out|4} the forms. Ms. Chen checked every {calculation|1}. Good {preparation|9} made the job easy. Mia was not married, so she didn't need to make a {joint|6} return with a {spouse|11}.

> 她們一起填寫了表格。陳小姐檢查了每一個計算。事前準備做得好，工作就變得很輕鬆。米雅還沒結婚，所以不需要和配偶一起合併申報。

Mia {filed|3} her taxes online two weeks early. "If you're late, there's a {penalty|8}," Ms. Chen said. A month later, Mia got a refund of $300!

> 米雅提早兩週就上網申報了。陳小姐說：「如果遲交，會有罰款喔。」一個月後，米雅拿到了 300 元的退稅！
