title: Mia's First Contract
title_zh: 米雅的第一份合約
---

Mia just started working at Green Leaf, a small tea company. Mr. Lin {established|7} the company ten years ago. On her first day, he gave her an important job: read a new {agreement|2} with a tea farm.

> 米雅剛開始在一家小茶葉公司「綠葉」上班。林先生十年前創辦了這家公司。上班第一天，他就交給她一項重要的工作：讀一份和茶園簽的新合約。

"Each {party|9} must {abide by|1} every rule in this contract," Mr. Lin said. "The farm is {obligated|8} to send us 200 boxes of tea every month, and we must pay within 30 days." Mia read every {provision|10} carefully. One rule was not clear, so she asked the farm to make it more {specific|12}.

> 林先生說：「每一方都必須遵守這份合約裡的每條規定。茶園有義務每個月送 200 箱茶給我們，我們則要在 30 天內付款。」米雅仔細讀了每一項條款。有一條規定寫得不清楚，所以她請茶園寫得更具體一點。

She also found a rule about {cancellation|4}: if either side wants to end the contract, it must give three months' notice. Mr. Lin decided to {engage|6} a lawyer to {determine|5} whether the rule was fair.

> 她還找到一條關於取消的規定：任何一方想終止合約，都必須提前三個月通知。林先生決定聘請一位律師，來判定這條規定是否公平。

The lawyer said it was fine, and the two sides {resolved|11} the last small problem by phone. The farm owner gave Mr. Lin his {assurance|3} that the tea would always arrive on time. Mr. Lin smiled at Mia. "Good work. You'll do well here."

> 律師說沒問題，雙方也在電話裡解決了最後一個小問題。茶園老闆向林先生保證，茶葉一定會準時送到。林先生對米雅笑著說：「做得好，妳在這裡會表現得很好。」
