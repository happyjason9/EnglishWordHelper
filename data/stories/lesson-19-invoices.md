---
title: The Wrong Invoice
title_zh: 開錯的發票
---

After the holidays, a big {customer|3}, a hotel, ordered 200 gift boxes. Mia first gave them an {estimate|7} of the total price. Because it was a big order, she offered a 15% {discount|4}.

> 假期過後，一家大客戶，也就是一間飯店，訂了 200 個禮盒。米雅先給了他們總價的估算。因為是大訂單，她給了八五折的折扣。

But when the hotel got the invoice, the manager called to {dispute|5} it. "You {charged|1} us the full price," he said. Mia checked and saw it was a {mistake|9}. The discount was missing.

> 但飯店收到發票後，經理打電話來提出異議：「你們向我們收了全額。」米雅查了一下，發現真的是錯誤，折扣漏掉了。

She said sorry and promised to {rectify|11} the error {promptly|10}. She sent a new invoice the same day. She also reminded him of the payment {terms|12}: pay within 30 days, or the company may {impose|8} a small late fee.

> 她道了歉，承諾會立即更正錯誤，當天就寄出新的發票。她也提醒經理付款條件：30 天內付款，否則公司可能會收取一筆小額的滯納金。

To be more {efficient|6}, Mia decided to {compile|2} all invoices in one computer file and check each one twice.

> 為了更有效率，米雅決定把所有發票整理在同一個電腦檔案裡，每一張都檢查兩次。
