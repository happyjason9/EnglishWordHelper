Mia wanted to buy a small apartment. She went to the bank to check her {balance|2}. She had saved enough money for a {down payment|7}, but she needed to {borrow|3} the rest.

> 米雅想買一間小公寓。她去銀行查了帳戶餘額。她存的錢夠付頭期款，但剩下的要用借的。

The bank officer explained the {mortgage|8}. "Every month, we will {deduct|5} the payment from your account," he said. Mia read the papers {cautiously|4} before she put her {signature|10} on them.

> 銀行行員向她說明房貸：「每個月我們會從您的帳戶扣除應繳的金額。」米雅小心地讀完文件，才在上面簽名。

Mia also had a savings account that paid her a small {dividend|6} every year. But the money in it was {restricted|9}: she could not {take out|11} more than a certain amount each month.

> 米雅還有一個儲蓄帳戶，每年會發給她一小筆紅利。但這個帳戶裡的錢是受限的：她每個月提領的金額不能超過一定的上限。

A week later, the bank called. "We {accept|1} your application," said the officer. The {transaction|12} was finished, and Mia got the keys to her new home.

> 一週後，銀行打電話來。行員說：「我們接受您的申請。」交易完成，米雅拿到了新家的鑰匙。
