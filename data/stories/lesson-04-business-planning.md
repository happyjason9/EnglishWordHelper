---
title: Plans for a Second Shop
title_zh: 開第二家店的計畫
---

Mr. Lin wanted to open a second shop. He asked his team to {gather|6} information about three possible places. Mia {evaluated|5} each place: How many people walk by? How high is the rent?

> 林先生想開第二家店。他請團隊收集三個候選地點的資料。米雅評估了每個地點：有多少人經過？房租多貴？

At the meeting, Mr. Lin gave a short {address|1} about the company's future. "Our {strategy|10} is simple," he said. "We want to {develop|4} slowly and {avoid|2} big mistakes. Every new shop is a {risk|9}."

> 在會議上，林先生針對公司的未來發表了簡短的演說。他說：「我們的策略很簡單：慢慢發展，避免犯大錯。每開一家新店都是一種風險。」

Mia used charts to {demonstrate|3} that the place near the train station was the best choice. The people there are {primarily|8} office workers who need tea every morning.

> 米雅用圖表說明，火車站附近的地點是最好的選擇。那裡的人主要是每天早上都需要喝茶的上班族。

A café owner nearby made an {offer|7}: he wanted to sell his old furniture cheaply. This would {strengthen|11} the plan because it saved money. There was only one small change: the {substitution|12} of a few broken chairs with new ones.

> 附近一家咖啡店的老闆提出一個方案：他想便宜賣掉店裡的舊家具。這能讓計畫更穩固，因為可以省錢。只有一個小改變：把幾張壞掉的椅子換成新的。
