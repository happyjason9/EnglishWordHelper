In December, Green Leaf {received|9} more orders than ever. Many customers chose gift boxes from the new online {catalog|3}. Mia had to make sure the company could {fulfill|4} every order.

> 十二月，綠葉收到的訂單比以往都多。很多客人從新的線上目錄挑選禮盒。米雅得確保公司能完成每一筆訂單。

First, Ken checked the {inventory|6}. They had 600 boxes {on hand|8}, but they needed 900. Mia called the box company and asked for more {supplies|12}.

> 首先，阿健清點了存貨。他們手邊有 600 盒，但需要 900 盒。米雅打電話給盒子公司，請他們多供應一些。

Then they chose a {carrier|2} to {ship|10} the boxes. Packing was an {integral|5} part of the job: each box had to be wrapped {sufficiently|11} so the tea cups inside would not break. Ken also checked every address {accurately|1}.

> 接著他們選了一家貨運公司來運送禮盒。包裝是這份工作不可或缺的一環：每個盒子都要包得夠牢，裡面的茶杯才不會破。阿健也仔細正確地核對每一個地址。

Thanks to their careful work, they {minimized|7} mistakes. Only two boxes out of 900 were late.

> 多虧他們細心工作，錯誤降到了最少。900 盒裡只有兩盒晚到。
