---
title: The Broken Kettle
title_zh: 壞掉的熱水壺
---

Green Leaf sells a {variety|12} of tea products, including electric kettles. One day, a customer called Mia. Her kettle stopped working after only eight months.

> 綠葉販賣各式各樣的茶類商品，包括電熱水壺。有一天，一位客人打電話給米雅。她的熱水壺才用八個月就壞了。

Mia checked the warranty. It {covers|4} all problems for one year, so it would not {expire|5} until spring. "Our warranty {protects|9} you," Mia said. "We {promise|8} to send you a new kettle."

> 米雅查了保固。保固涵蓋一年內的所有問題，所以要到春天才會到期。米雅說：「我們的保固會保障您，我們承諾寄一個新的熱水壺給您。」

The warranty {requires|11} customers to show a receipt. The woman found hers quickly. Mia asked how often she used the kettle. "{Frequently|6} — maybe ten times a day," she said. Mia did not want to {imply|7} that the customer had done something wrong. After all, making tea many times a day is a {characteristic|1} of tea lovers!

> 保固要求客人出示收據，那位女士很快就找到了。米雅問她多常用這個熱水壺。她說：「很常用，大概一天十次吧。」米雅不想暗示是客人做錯了什麼。畢竟一天泡很多次茶，正是愛茶人的特徵！

Mr. Lin {considered|3} the problem. "If we don't help her, the {consequence|2} could be bad for us," he said. "A good {reputation|10} is worth more than one kettle."

> 林先生考慮了這個問題。他說：「如果我們不幫她，後果可能對我們很不利。好的聲譽比一個熱水壺值錢多了。」
