---
title: The Tea Conference
title_zh: 茶業研討會
---

Every year, the Tea Sellers' {Association|3} {holds|6} a big conference. This year, Mr. Lin asked Mia to {attend|4} with him. Mia made all the travel {arrangements|2}.

> 茶商協會每年都會舉辦一場大型研討會。今年林先生請米雅跟他一起參加。米雅負責所有的旅行安排。

First, she had to {register|9} for the event online. Then she looked for a hotel. The first hotel could only {accommodate|1} fifty guests, and it was already full. So she {selected|10} a quiet hotel close to the conference {location|7}.

> 首先，她得上網報名活動。接著她找飯店。第一家飯店只能容納五十位客人，而且已經客滿了。所以她選了一家離會場很近的安靜飯店。

On the first day, the main hall was {overcrowded|8}. More than five hundred people came! Mia and Mr. Lin went to a smaller {session|11} about Japanese tea instead. Mia was brave and {took part in|12} the discussion.

> 第一天，主會場擠得水洩不通，來了超過五百人！米雅和林先生改去參加一個關於日本茶的小場次。米雅鼓起勇氣參與了討論。

There she met a tea seller from Kyoto. They exchanged business cards and promised to {get in touch|5} after the conference.

> 她在那裡認識了一位來自京都的茶商。他們交換了名片，約好研討會結束後保持聯絡。
