title: Asking for a Raise
title_zh: 要求加薪
---

Mia had worked at Green Leaf for one year. She was {aware|1} that her {wage|12} was lower than other office workers in the city. She wanted to ask for a {raise|9}, but she was nervous.

> 米雅在綠葉工作滿一年了。她知道自己的薪水比城裡其他上班族低。她想要求加薪，但很緊張。

She brought up the topic {delicately|5}. "I've taken on many new jobs this year," she said. "I hope the company can {compensate|4} me for the extra work." Mr. Lin listened. He agreed that her hard work was a good {basis|2} for higher pay.

> 她很有技巧地提起這件事：「我今年接下了很多新工作，希望公司能補償我多做的部分。」林先生聽完後，同意她的努力是加薪的好理由。

They {negotiated|8} for a while. In the end, Mr. Lin gave her a 10% raise. She also became {eligible|6} for the company's health {benefits|3}, and she could now work {flexibly|7} from home one day a week.

> 他們談了一陣子。最後林先生給她加薪百分之十。她也開始有資格享有公司的健康福利，而且現在每週可以彈性在家工作一天。

Mr. Lin also explained the pension plan. "After five years, your pension will be fully {vested|11}," he said. "Then the money is yours when you {retire|10}."

> 林先生也說明了退休金計畫：「滿五年之後，妳的退休金權利就完全確定了。等妳退休時，這筆錢就是妳的。」
