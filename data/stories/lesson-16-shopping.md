As the new sales manager, Mia wanted to learn about shoppers' {behavior|3}. She and Ken went to a big shopping mall to {explore|7} the newest {trends|12}.

> 身為新的業務經理，米雅想了解消費者的行為。她和阿健去了一家大型購物中心，探索最新的潮流。

The mall was very busy. Ken could not {bear|2} the noise and the crowds. Mia {comforted|5} him: "Just one more hour!" They looked at the {merchandise|10} in many tea and gift shops. Some shops were trying to {expand|6} into healthy drinks.

> 購物中心人潮洶湧。阿健受不了噪音和人群，米雅安慰他：「再一個小時就好！」他們看了很多茶行和禮品店的商品。有些店正努力把生意擴展到健康飲品。

In one shop, a customer tried to {bargain|1} for a lower price, but the shop {strictly|11} followed its price list. At the {checkout|4}, a sign said: "Bags are not free. Paying for bags is {mandatory|9}."

> 在一家店裡，有位客人想殺價，但店家嚴格照價目表賣。結帳處有個牌子寫著：「袋子不免費，購買袋子為強制規定。」

Mia bought a few {items|8} to study. She saw that people loved small, pretty gift boxes. "Let's sell our tea in gift boxes," she told Ken.

> 米雅買了幾樣商品回去研究。她發現大家都很喜歡小巧漂亮的禮盒。她對阿健說：「我們也來賣茶葉禮盒吧。」
