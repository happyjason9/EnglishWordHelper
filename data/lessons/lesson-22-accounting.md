## 1. accounting (n.) — 會計,帳務

- a. Good accounting is needed in all businesses.
- a-zh. 所有企業都需要良好的會計作業。
- b. There are a number of good software programs to help with both personal and business accounting.
- b-zh. 有不少好用的軟體可以協助處理個人與企業的帳務。
- 衍生: accountant 會計師;account 帳戶
- 搭配: accounting department 會計部

## 2. accumulate (v.) — 累積

- a. They have accumulated more than enough information.
- a-zh. 他們已經累積了綽綽有餘的資料。
- b. The bills started to accumulate after the secretary quit.
- b-zh. 祕書離職後,帳單開始越堆越多。
- 同義: gather、collect、build up
- 衍生: accumulation 累積

## 3. asset (n.) — 資產

- a. The company's assets are worth millions of dollars.
- a-zh. 這家公司的資產價值數百萬美元。
- b. A sophisticated accounting system is an asset to a company.
- b-zh. 一套完善的會計系統是公司的寶貴資產。
- 用法: be an asset to sb/sth 對⋯是寶貴的人才或資產
- 反義: liability 負債

## 4. audit (n./v.) — 審計,查帳;審核

- a. No one looks forward to an audit by the government.
- a-zh. 沒有人會期待被政府查帳。
- b. The independent accountants audited the company's books.
- b-zh. 獨立會計師審核了這家公司的帳冊。
- 衍生: auditor 審計員
- 搭配: tax audit 稅務稽查

## 5. budget (n./v.) — 預算;編預算

- a. The department head was pleased that she received a 10 percent increase in her budget.
- a-zh. 部門主管很高興她的預算增加了百分之十。
- b. The company will have to budget more money for this department next year.
- b-zh. 公司明年必須為這個部門編列更多預算。
- 搭配: on a budget 預算有限;over budget 超出預算
- 用法: budget for sth 為某事編列預算

## 6. build up (v. 片語) — 發展

- a. The firm has built up a solid reputation for itself.
- a-zh. 這家公司已經為自己建立起穩固的聲譽。
- a-cloze: built up
- b. Be careful. Your inventory of parts is building up.
- b-zh. 小心點,你的零件庫存越積越多了。
- b-cloze: building up
- 拼字: 不規則變化 build–built–built
- 衍生: buildup 累積、增加(n.)

## 7. client (n.) — 客人

- a. The accountant attracted many new clients through her website.
- a-zh. 這位會計師透過她的網站吸引了許多新客戶。
- b. Maintaining close contact with clients keeps the account managers aware of changing needs.
- b-zh. 與客戶保持密切聯繫,讓客戶經理能掌握不斷變化的需求。
- 同義: customer
- 衍生: clientele 客戶群

## 8. debt (n.) — 借款,負債

- a. The company has been very careful and is slowly digging itself out of debt.
- a-zh. 這家公司一直很謹慎,正慢慢擺脫債務。
- b. The banks are worried about your increasing debt.
- b-zh. 銀行很擔心你不斷增加的債務。
- 發音: b 不發音 /det/
- 搭配: in debt 負債;pay off a debt 還清債務

## 9. outstanding (adj.) — 未償付的

- a. That client still has several outstanding bills.
- a-zh. 那位客戶還有好幾張帳單沒付。
- b. Clients with outstanding bills will not receive further service until the bills are paid.
- b-zh. 有未付帳單的客戶,在付清之前不會再獲得服務。
- 多義: 另有「優秀的、傑出的」之意,如 an outstanding performance
- 同義: unpaid、overdue

## 10. profitably (adv.) — 有利地

- a. The company used its accountant's advice profitably.
- a-zh. 這家公司善用了會計師的建議而獲利。
- b. We invested in the stock market profitably.
- b-zh. 我們投資股市有所獲利。
- 衍生: profit 利潤;profitable 有利可圖的;profitability 獲利能力

## 11. reconcile (v.) — 對帳

- a. The client uses his bank statements to reconcile his accounts.
- a-zh. 這位客戶用銀行對帳單來核對他的帳目。
- b. The accountant found the error when she reconciled the account.
- b-zh. 會計師在對帳時發現了錯誤。
- 多義: 另有「使和解、調和」之意
- 衍生: reconciliation 對帳、和解

## 12. turnover (n.) — 營業額,人員流動率

- a. We have had a low turnover of inventory this month and didn't bring in much money.
- a-zh. 我們這個月的存貨週轉率很低,沒賺到多少錢。
- b. Because of our high employee turnover we actually spend less money on salaries.
- b-zh. 由於員工流動率高,我們花在薪資上的錢其實比較少。
- 搭配: employee turnover 員工流動率;inventory turnover 存貨週轉率
- 延伸: 英式用法常指「營業額」
