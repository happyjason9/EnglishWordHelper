## 1. assemble (v.) — 集合、彙整;組裝

- a. Her assistant copied and assembled the documents.
- a-zh. 她的助理把文件影印並彙整起來。
- b. The mail room clerk read the directions before assembling the parts to the new postage printer.
- b-zh. 收發室職員在組裝新郵資印表機的零件前先讀了說明。
- 同義: put together、bring together
- 衍生: assembly 集會、組裝;assembly line 生產線

## 2. beforehand (adv.) — 預先、事先

- a. To speed up the mailing, we should prepare the labels beforehand.
- a-zh. 為加快郵寄速度,我們應該事先準備好標籤。
- b. The goods could have been shipped today had they given us the order beforehand.
- b-zh. 如果他們事先把訂單給我們,貨今天就能出了。
- 同義: in advance、in anticipation
- 用法: 置於句尾的副詞,不接名詞

## 3. complication (n.) — 困難、複雜的情況

- a. There was a complication with the delivery because the address was written incorrectly.
- a-zh. 因為地址寫錯了,配送出現了狀況。
- b. Complications always arise when we try to cover too many topics in one letter.
- b-zh. 當我們想在一封信裡涵蓋太多主題時,總會出現複雜的問題。
- 衍生: complicate 使複雜;complicated 複雜的
- 搭配: arise / cause complications

## 4. courier (n.) — 快遞員、快遞

- a. We hired a courier to deliver the package.
- a-zh. 我們雇了一位快遞來送這個包裹。
- b. The courier service will clear the goods through customs.
- b-zh. 這家快遞公司會替貨物辦理通關。
- 搭配: courier service 快遞服務;by courier 以快遞方式
- 同義: messenger、official delivery person

## 5. distribute (v.) — 分配、分發

- a. We no longer distribute our newsletter by mail because everyone reads it online.
- a-zh. 我們不再以郵寄方式分發電子報,因為大家都在線上閱讀。
- b. We plan to distribute copies of the announcement throughout the building.
- b-zh. 我們打算把公告的影本分送到整棟大樓各處。
- 衍生: distribution 分發、配銷;distributor 經銷商
- 用法: distribute sth to sb 把某物分發給某人

## 6. express (adj.) — 迅速的、快遞的

- a. It's important that this document be there tomorrow, so please send it express mail.
- a-zh. 這份文件明天務必送達,所以請用快遞寄出。
- b. Express mail costs more than regular mail service, but it is more efficient.
- b-zh. 快遞郵件比一般郵遞貴,但效率更高。
- 搭配: express mail / delivery / service
- 延伸: 當動詞時意為「表達」,express an opinion

## 7. fold (v.) — 摺疊

- a. Fold the letter into three parts before stuffing it into the envelope.
- a-zh. 把信紙摺成三摺再塞進信封。
- b. Don't fold the document if it doesn't fit the envelope.
- b-zh. 如果文件放不進信封,也不要把它摺起來。
- 搭配: fold in half / into three 摺成兩摺或三摺
- 反義: unfold 展開

## 8. layout (n.) — 設計、安排;版面配置

- a. We had to change the layout when we changed the size of the paper.
- a-zh. 我們更改紙張尺寸時,必須跟著調整版面配置。
- b. The layout for the new brochure was submitted by the designer.
- b-zh. 新手冊的版面設計已由設計師提交。
- 拼字: 名詞寫在一起 layout;動詞分開 lay out
- 搭配: page / print / product layout

## 9. mention (n./v.) — 提及、提到

- a. There was no mention of the cost in the proposal.
- a-zh. 提案中完全沒有提到成本。
- b. You should mention in the letter that we can arrange for mailing the brochures as well as printing them.
- b-zh. 你應該在信中提到,我們除了印製手冊外也能安排寄送。
- 搭配: make mention of sth;not to mention 更不用說
- 用法: mention sth to sb,不說 mention sb sth

## 10. petition (n./v.) — 請願書;向⋯請願

- a. The petition was photocopied and distributed to workers who will collect the necessary signatures.
- a-zh. 這份請願書已影印並分發給負責收集必要簽名的員工。
- b. We petitioned the postal officials to start delivering mail twice a day in business areas.
- b-zh. 我們向郵政官員請願,要求在商業區每天送兩次信。
- 用法: petition sb to V 向某人請願做⋯
- 衍生: petitioner 請願人

## 11. proof (v.) — 校對

- a. This letter was not proofed very carefully; it is full of errors.
- a-zh. 這封信沒有仔細校對,錯誤百出。
- b. Please proof the memo one more time before you distribute it.
- b-zh. 分發這份備忘錄前請再校對一次。
- 同義: proofread、look for errors
- 延伸: 當名詞時意為證據、證明

## 12. revise (v.) — 修訂、改寫

- a. The brochure was revised several times before it was sent to the printer.
- a-zh. 這本手冊在送印前修改了好幾次。
- b. We will need to revise the form letter since our address has changed.
- b-zh. 由於我們地址變更,必須修改這份制式信函。
- 衍生: revision 修訂版、修改
- 同義: rewrite、amend、edit
