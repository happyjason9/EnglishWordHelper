## 1. achievement (n.) — 完成、成就

- a. Your main achievements will be listed in your personnel file.
- a-zh. 你的主要成就會列入你的人事檔案。
- b. Joseph's achievements in R&D will go down in company history.
- b-zh. 約瑟夫在研發部門的成就將會載入公司史冊。
- 搭配: a sense of achievement 成就感
- 衍生: achieve 達成;achiever 有成就的人

## 2. contribute (v.) — 貢獻、捐獻

- a. Make sure your boss is aware of the work you contributed to the project.
- a-zh. 務必讓你的老闆知道你為這個專案貢獻了哪些工作。
- b. All employees are asked to contribute a few minutes of their spare time to clean up the office.
- b-zh. 所有員工都被要求撥出幾分鐘空閒時間來打掃辦公室。
- 用法: contribute to sth 對⋯有貢獻;contribute A to B 把 A 貢獻給 B
- 衍生: contribution 貢獻;contributor 貢獻者

## 3. dedication (n.) — 致力、奉獻

- a. The director's dedication to a high-quality product has motivated many of his employees.
- a-zh. 主管對高品質產品的投入,激勵了許多員工。
- b. We would never be where we are today if it weren't for many long hours and so much dedication.
- b-zh. 要不是有許多長時間的付出與無比的奉獻,我們不會有今天的成就。
- 用法: dedication to sth 對⋯的奉獻
- 衍生: dedicate 奉獻;dedicated 專注投入的

## 4. look forward to (v.) — 期盼

- a. The regional director was looking forward to the new, larger offices.
- a-zh. 區域主管期待著新的、更大的辦公室。
- a-cloze: looking forward to
- b. We look forward to seeing you at the next meeting.
- b-zh. 我們期待在下次會議上見到你。
- 注意: to 是介系詞,後接 V-ing 或名詞,不接原形動詞
- 同義: anticipate、be eager for

## 5. look to (v.) — 倚賴

- a. The workers always looked to him to settle their disagreements.
- a-zh. 員工們總是倚賴他來調解彼此的紛爭。
- a-cloze: looked to
- b. The staff is looking to their supervisor for guidance and direction.
- b-zh. 員工們指望主管提供指引與方向。
- b-cloze: looking to
- 用法: look to sb for sth 指望某人提供⋯
- 同義: depend on、rely on

## 6. loyal (adj.) — 忠實的

- a. You have been such a loyal advisor for so many years, I'm not sure what I'll do without you.
- a-zh. 你這麼多年來一直是如此忠實的顧問,我不知道沒有你我該怎麼辦。
- b. Even though your assistant is loyal, you have to question his job performance.
- b-zh. 即使你的助理很忠誠,你還是得質疑他的工作表現。
- 用法: be loyal to sb / sth 對⋯忠誠
- 衍生: loyalty 忠誠;反義 disloyal 不忠的

## 7. merit (n.) — 優點

- a. Employees are evaluated on their merit and not on seniority.
- a-zh. 員工是依據優異的表現來評量,而不是依年資。
- b. Your work has improved tremendously and is of great merit.
- b-zh. 你的工作表現進步極大,非常出色。
- 搭配: on merit 依功績;merit-based 以功績為基礎的
- 對照: seniority 年資,與 merit 常成對比

## 8. obviously (adv.) — 明顯地

- a. Her tardiness was obviously resented by her coworkers.
- a-zh. 她的遲到顯然讓同事們很不滿。
- b. He was obviously working hard to get the promotion.
- b-zh. 他顯然為了爭取升遷而努力工作。
- 同義: clearly、evidently
- 衍生: obvious 明顯的

## 9. praise (n./v.) — 讚美、表揚

- a. John received a lot of praise for the article he wrote about business trends.
- a-zh. 約翰寫的商業趨勢文章獲得許多讚賞。
- b. It never hurts to praise an employee for a job well done.
- b-zh. 稱讚員工把工作做得很好,從來沒有壞處。
- 搭配: praise sb for sth 因⋯稱讚某人;in praise of 讚揚
- 衍生: praiseworthy 值得稱讚的

## 10. promote (v.) — 晉升、促進

- a. Even though the sales associate had a good year, it wasn't possible to promote him.
- a-zh. 儘管這位業務專員這一年表現很好,但還是不可能晉升他。
- b. The assistant director promoted the idea that the director was incompetent.
- b-zh. 副主管大力宣揚主管不稱職的說法。
- 衍生: promotion 晉升、促銷;promoter 發起人
- 延伸: 也指推銷,promote a product

## 11. recognition (n.) — 讚賞、肯定

- a. The president's assistant was finally given the recognition that she has deserved for many years.
- a-zh. 總裁助理終於得到她多年來應得的肯定。
- b. Recognition of excellent work should be routine for every manager.
- b-zh. 肯定優秀的工作表現,應該成為每位主管的例行公事。
- 搭配: in recognition of 為表彰⋯;gain recognition 受到肯定
- 衍生: recognize 認出、承認

## 12. value (v.) — 重視、估價

- a. Employees value their colleagues' opinions.
- a-zh. 員工重視同事的意見。
- b. The expert valued the text at $7,000.
- b-zh. 專家把這份文稿估價為七千美元。
- 衍生: valuable 貴重的;valued 受重視的;invaluable 無價的
- 注意: 當名詞時意為價值
