## 1. calculation (n.) — 計算

- a. It took my accountant some time to complete the calculations on my income tax.
- a-zh. 我的會計師花了一些時間才算完我的所得稅。
- b. According to my calculations, I'll owe less money on my income taxes this year.
- b-zh. 根據我的計算,我今年要繳的所得稅會比較少。
- 衍生: calculate 計算;calculator 計算機
- 搭配: income tax 所得稅

## 2. deadline (n.) — 截止期限

- a. The deadline for paying this year's taxes is just two weeks away.
- a-zh. 今年繳稅的截止期限只剩兩週了。
- b. My best work is done with strict deadlines.
- b-zh. 在嚴格的期限下,我能做出最好的成果。
- 搭配: meet a deadline 趕上期限;miss a deadline 錯過期限

## 3. file (v.) — 提交

- a. If you file your taxes late, you will have to pay a fine.
- a-zh. 如果你逾期報稅,就得繳罰款。
- b. If you believe the tax preparer gave you incorrect information, you should file a complaint.
- b-zh. 如果你認為報稅代理人給了你錯誤的資訊,你應該提出申訴。
- 多義: file a lawsuit 提起訴訟;名詞為「文件、檔案」
- 搭配: file taxes 報稅;file a complaint 提出申訴

## 4. fill out (v. 片語) — 填寫

- a. I usually ask someone to help me fill out my tax form.
- a-zh. 我通常會請人幫我填寫報稅表格。
- b. Don't forget to sign the tax form after you have filled it out.
- b-zh. 填好報稅表後別忘了簽名。
- b-cloze: filled it out
- 用法: 代名詞要放中間:fill it out
- 同義: complete、fill in

## 5. give up (v. 片語) — 放棄

- a. Bruce gave up paying an accountant to prepare his tax return and now does it himself.
- a-zh. 布魯斯不再花錢請會計師幫他準備報稅,現在都自己來。
- a-cloze: gave up
- b. Ms. Gomez gave up hope that she would ever receive a large tax refund.
- b-zh. 戈梅茲女士已經不再指望能拿到一大筆退稅。
- b-cloze: gave up
- 用法: give up + V-ing 放棄做某事
- 拼字: 不規則變化 give–gave–given

## 6. joint (adj.) — 共同的

- a. We opened a joint bank account five years ago.
- a-zh. 我們五年前開了一個聯名銀行帳戶。
- b. The couple no longer files joint tax returns.
- b-zh. 這對夫妻不再合併申報所得稅了。
- 搭配: joint account 聯名帳戶;joint venture 合資企業
- 衍生: jointly 共同地

## 7. owe (v.) — 欠(錢)

- a. People are surprised to discover that they owe money in income taxes at the end of the year.
- a-zh. 人們到了年底才驚訝地發現自己還欠所得稅。
- b. As the business grew, the owner paid back loans and owed less money.
- b-zh. 隨著事業成長,老闆還清了貸款,欠的錢也變少了。
- 用法: owe sb sth 欠某人某物;owe A to B 把 A 歸功於 B
- 片語: owing to 由於

## 8. penalty (n.) — 處罰,罰款

- a. Anyone who pays less than they should in taxes will face a penalty.
- a-zh. 任何少繳稅的人都會面臨罰款。
- b. Penalties are imposed to discourage underpayment of taxes.
- b-zh. 祭出罰則是為了遏止少繳稅款。
- 拼字: 複數 penalties
- 衍生: penalize 處罰

## 9. preparation (n.) — 準備

- a. Income tax preparation can take a long time.
- a-zh. 準備所得稅申報可能要花很長的時間。
- b. You should do some preparation on your own before hiring an accountant to work on your taxes.
- b-zh. 在請會計師處理稅務之前,你應該自己先做些準備。
- 衍生: prepare 準備;preparer 準備者
- 搭配: in preparation for 為⋯做準備

## 10. refund (n./v.) — 退款;退還

- a. With the tax refund, we bought two plane tickets.
- a-zh. 我們用退稅買了兩張機票。
- b. The government will refund any money that you overpaid.
- b-zh. 政府會退還你多繳的任何款項。
- 易混: refund 退款;fund 資金
- 搭配: tax refund 退稅;full refund 全額退款

## 11. spouse (n.) — 配偶

- a. You can claim your spouse as a dependent on your tax return if he doesn't earn an income.
- a-zh. 如果你的配偶沒有收入,你可以在報稅時把他列為扶養親屬。
- b. My spouse prepares the tax return for both of us.
- b-zh. 我的配偶幫我們兩個人準備所得稅申報。
- 搭配: tax return 所得稅申報表;income 收入
- 延伸: dependent 受扶養人

## 12. withhold (v.) — 不給,保留

- a. My employer withholds money from each paycheck to apply toward my income taxes.
- a-zh. 我的雇主每次發薪都會預扣一筆錢,用來繳我的所得稅。
- b. Do not withhold information from your accountant, or he won't be able to prepare your taxes correctly.
- b-zh. 不要對你的會計師隱瞞資訊,否則他沒辦法正確地幫你報稅。
- 用法: withhold A from B 對 B 扣留/隱瞞 A
- 拼字: 不規則變化 withhold–withheld–withheld
- 衍生: withholding tax 預扣稅
