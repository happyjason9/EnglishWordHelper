## 1. attract (v.) — 吸引

- a. The new packaging attracted many first-time buyers.
- a-zh. 新包裝吸引了許多首次購買的顧客。
- b. Discount coupons attract price-sensitive customers.
- b-zh. 折價券吸引對價格敏感的顧客。
- 衍生: attractive 有吸引力的;attraction 吸引力、景點

## 2. compare (v.) — 比較

- a. Customers often compare prices online before visiting the store.
- a-zh. 顧客通常會先上網比價再到店裡。
- b. Our delivery time is short compared to other suppliers.
- b-zh. 與其他供應商相比,我們的交貨時間較短。
- 片語: compare A with/to B;compared to/with 與⋯相比

## 3. competition (n.) — 競賽、競爭

- a. The two brands are in fierce competition for young consumers.
- a-zh. 兩個品牌正激烈爭奪年輕消費者。
- b. We lowered our prices to stay ahead of the competition.
- b-zh. 我們調降價格以領先競爭對手。
- 用法: the competition 可指「競爭對手(統稱)」
- 衍生: compete 競爭;competitive 有競爭力的;competitor 競爭者

## 4. consume (v.) — 耗費、耗盡;消費

- a. The ad campaign consumed nearly half of the annual budget.
- a-zh. 這波廣告活動耗費了將近一半的年度預算。
- b. Younger consumers consume most of their media online.
- b-zh. 年輕消費者大部分的媒體消費都在網路上。
- 衍生: consumer 消費者;consumption 消耗、消費量

## 5. convince (v.) — 說服、使相信

- a. The ad needs to convince viewers that the product is worth the price.
- a-zh. 廣告必須說服觀眾這項產品值這個價格。
- b. It took a strong testimonial to convince skeptical buyers.
- b-zh. 需要有力的推薦見證才能說服存疑的買家。
- 用法: convince sb. that/of sth;convince sb. to V
- 易混: persuade 較強調「說服去做某事」,convince 較強調「使相信」

## 6. currently (adv.) — 目前、現今

- a. The brand is currently running a buy-one-get-one promotion.
- a-zh. 該品牌目前正在推行買一送一的促銷活動。
- b. Currently, most ad spending goes to social media platforms.
- b-zh. 目前大部分的廣告預算都投入社群媒體平台。
- 注意: 課本原文誤植成「參與、涉入」,正確意思是「目前、現今」,等同 at present
- 易混: currency(貨幣)是完全不同的字

## 7. fad (n.) — 一時的流行、熱潮

- a. Retailers worry that the product is just a passing fad.
- a-zh. 零售商擔心這項產品只是曇花一現的流行。
- b. Unlike most fads, this trend has lasted for several years.
- b-zh. 不同於大多數的流行,這股風潮已經持續好幾年。
- 易混: fashion 泛指時尚,fad 特指「短暫、來得快去得也快」的流行

## 8. inspiration (n.) — 靈感;啟發的來源

- a. The campaign drew its inspiration from local street art.
- a-zh. 這次的廣告活動從當地街頭藝術中汲取靈感。
- b. Her success became an inspiration for the whole sales team.
- b-zh. 她的成功成為整個業務團隊的激勵來源。
- 衍生: inspire 啟發、激勵;inspiring 鼓舞人心的

## 9. market (v./n.) — 行銷、推銷;市場需求

- a. The company markets its products mainly through influencers.
- a-zh. 公司主要透過網紅來行銷產品。
- b. There is a growing market for eco-friendly packaging.
- b-zh. 環保包裝的市場需求正在成長。
- 衍生: marketing 行銷;marketable 有市場價值的

## 10. persuasion (n.) — 說服力

- a. Good copywriting relies heavily on the art of persuasion.
- a-zh. 好的文案很仰賴說服的藝術。
- b. The team used persuasion rather than discounts to close the deal.
- b-zh. 團隊靠說服而非折扣來成交。
- 衍生: persuade 說服(動詞);persuasive 有說服力的

## 11. productive (adj.) — 有成效的、多產的

- a. The new ad format proved highly productive for lead generation.
- a-zh. 新的廣告形式在開發潛在客戶上非常有成效。
- b. A productive marketing team can do more with a smaller budget.
- b-zh. 有效率的行銷團隊能用較少預算做出更多成果。
- 衍生: produce 生產;production 生產、製作;productivity 生產力

## 12. satisfaction (n.) — 滿意

- a. Customer satisfaction scores rose after the redesign.
- a-zh. 改版後顧客滿意度分數提升了。
- b. We offer a full refund to guarantee your satisfaction.
- b-zh. 我們提供全額退費以保證您的滿意度。
- 搭配: customer satisfaction 顧客滿意度;to sb.'s satisfaction 令某人滿意
- 衍生: satisfy 使滿意;satisfied 感到滿意的;satisfactory 令人滿意的
