## 1. ability (n.) — 能力

- a. The designer's ability was obvious from her portfolio.
- a-zh. 從這位設計師的作品集就看得出她的能力。
- b. The ability to work with others is a key requirement.
- b-zh. 與他人共事的能力是一項關鍵要求。
- 用法: the ability to V,後接不定詞
- 同義: a skill、a competence

## 2. apply (v.) — 申請、應用

- a. The college graduate applied for three jobs and received three offers.
- a-zh. 這位大學畢業生應徵了三份工作,收到三個錄取通知。
- b. Everyone who is interested should apply in person at any branch office.
- b-zh. 有興趣者皆可親自前往任一分公司應徵。
- 用法: apply for a job 應徵工作;apply to a school 申請學校
- 衍生: applicant 應徵者;application 申請書

## 3. background (n.) — 背景、經歷

- a. Your background in the publishing industry is a definite asset for this job.
- a-zh. 你在出版業的背景,對這份工作絕對是加分。
- b. The employer did a complete background check before offering him the job.
- b-zh. 雇主在錄取他之前做了完整的背景調查。
- 搭配: background check 背景調查;a background in sth
- 同義: a person's experience, education, and family history

## 4. call in (v.) — 召來、召喚

- a. The young woman was so excited when she was called in for an interview that she told everyone she knew.
- a-zh. 這位年輕女子接到面試通知時非常興奮,告訴了所有認識的人。
- a-cloze: called in
- b. The human resources manager called in all the qualified applicants for a second interview.
- b-zh. 人資經理把所有合格的應徵者都找來進行第二次面試。
- b-cloze: called in
- 用法: 可分離片語:call her in
- 同義: to ask to come、to beckon

## 5. confidence (n.) — 自信

- a. Good applicants show confidence during an interview.
- a-zh. 優秀的應徵者在面試時會展現自信。
- b. He had too much confidence and thought that the job was his.
- b-zh. 他過於自信,以為這份工作非他莫屬。
- 同義: a belief in one's abilities、self-esteem
- 衍生: confident 有自信的;confidential 機密的

## 6. constantly (adv.) — 不斷地

- a. The company is constantly looking for highly trained employees.
- a-zh. 這家公司不斷在尋找訓練有素的員工。
- b. Martin constantly checked his messages to see if anyone had called for an interview.
- b-zh. 馬丁不斷查看訊息,看有沒有人通知他面試。
- 同義: on a continual basis、happening all the time
- 衍生: constant 持續的、不變的

## 7. expert (n.) — 專家

- a. Our department head is an expert in financing.
- a-zh. 我們的部門主管是融資方面的專家。
- b. The candidate demonstrated that he was an expert in marketing.
- b-zh. 這位候選人展現出自己是行銷方面的專家。
- 用法: an expert in / on / at sth
- 衍生: expertise 專業知識;expertly 熟練地

## 8. follow up (v./n.) — 後續接著做;後續行動

- a. Always follow up an interview with a thank-you note.
- a-zh. 面試後一定要寄感謝函作為後續聯繫。
- b. As a follow-up, the candidate sent the company a list of references.
- b-zh. 作為後續動作,這位候選人寄給公司一份推薦人名單。
- b-cloze: follow-up
- 拼字: 動詞分開 follow up;名詞加連字號 follow-up
- 用法: follow up on sth 追蹤某事的後續

## 9. hesitant (adj.) — 遲疑的

- a. Marla was hesitant about negotiating a higher salary.
- a-zh. 瑪拉對於爭取更高的薪水感到遲疑。
- b. The recent college graduate was hesitant about accepting his first offer.
- b-zh. 這位剛畢業的大學生對於接受第一個錄取通知有所遲疑。
- 用法: be hesitant about / to V 對⋯遲疑
- 衍生: hesitate 猶豫;hesitation 猶豫

## 10. interview (n./v.) — 面試、訪談;面試、採訪

- a. It is very important to act professionally during a job interview.
- a-zh. 面試時表現專業非常重要。
- b. We had to interview a lot of people before we found the right one for the job.
- b-zh. 我們面試了很多人,才找到適合這份工作的人選。
- 衍生: interviewer 面試官;interviewee 受試者
- 同義: a meeting for getting information

## 11. present (v.) — 介紹、呈現

- a. The human resources director presents each candidate's résumé to the department supervisor for review.
- a-zh. 人資主管把每位候選人的履歷呈給部門主管審閱。
- b. The candidate presented her qualifications so well that the employer offered her a job on the spot.
- b-zh. 這位候選人把自己的條件呈現得極好,雇主當場就錄取了她。
- 注意: 動詞重音在後 /prɪˈzent/;名詞重音在前 /ˈprezənt/
- 衍生: presentation 簡報;presenter 發表者

## 12. weakly (adv.) — 虛弱地、薄弱地

- a. Her hands trembled and she spoke weakly at the interview.
- a-zh. 她在面試時雙手發抖,說話有氣無力。
- b. She wrote so weakly we couldn't read it.
- b-zh. 她字寫得太淡,我們看不清楚。
- 易混: weakly 虛弱地;weekly 每週地,發音相同
- 衍生: weak 虛弱的;weaken 使變弱;weakness 弱點
