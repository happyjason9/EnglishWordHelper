## 1. charge (v./n.) — 收費;費用

- a. The customer service representative was responsible for telling all existing customers that higher prices would be charged next month.
- a-zh. 客服代表負責告知所有現有客戶,下個月將收取較高的價格。
- b. The extra charge for gift wrapping your purchase will appear on your invoice.
- b-zh. 禮品包裝的額外費用會出現在你的發票上。
- 搭配: extra charge 額外費用;free of charge 免費;in charge of 負責
- 用法: charge sb for sth 就某事向某人收費
- 衍生: surcharge 附加費;overcharge 多收費

## 2. compile (v.) — 收集、編輯

- a. I have compiled a list of the most popular items for sale on our website.
- a-zh. 我已經整理出一份我們網站上最熱賣商品的清單。
- b. The clerk is responsible for compiling the orders at the end of the day.
- b-zh. 這位職員負責在每天結束時彙整訂單。
- 同義: collect、assemble、put together
- 衍生: compilation 彙編;compiler 編輯者、編譯器
- 延伸: 電腦領域指「編譯」

## 3. customer (n.) — 顧客

- a. Let's make sure all invoices sent to customers are kept in alphabetical order.
- a-zh. 我們要確保寄給客戶的所有發票都按字母順序保存。
- b. Our customer satisfaction plan offers a discount to customers who pay their invoices on time.
- b-zh. 我們的客戶滿意計畫,對準時繳清發票的客戶提供折扣。
- 搭配: customer service 客戶服務;customer satisfaction 客戶滿意度
- 同義: client 客戶;consumer 消費者
- 衍生: customize 客製化

## 4. discount (n.) — 折扣

- a. Customers who pay their invoices before the end of the month receive a 5 percent discount.
- a-zh. 在月底前付清發票的客戶可享有百分之五的折扣。
- b. The discount on the sale items was not very big.
- b-zh. 特價商品的折扣不是很大。
- 搭配: at a discount 以折扣價;a 10 percent discount 九折
- 延伸: 也可當動詞,意為打折

## 5. dispute (v.) — 爭論、質疑、不贊成

- a. Customers may dispute certain charges on an invoice.
- a-zh. 客戶可以對發票上的某些費用提出異議。
- b. A customer called the company to dispute the no returns policy.
- b-zh. 有位客戶打電話給公司,對不接受退貨的政策提出質疑。
- 搭配: dispute a charge 對費用提出異議
- 衍生: disputable 有爭議的;indisputable 無可爭辯的
- 延伸: 當名詞時指爭議、糾紛

## 6. efficient (adj.) — 有效率的

- a. The accountant was so efficient in processing the receipts that she had the job done by lunch.
- a-zh. 這位會計處理收據非常有效率,午餐前就把工作做完了。
- b. Electronic invoicing has helped us to be efficient.
- b-zh. 電子發票幫助我們提高了效率。
- 易混: efficient 指有效率;effective 指有效果
- 衍生: efficiency 效率;efficiently 有效率地;inefficient 沒效率的

## 7. estimate (v./n.) — 估計

- a. We estimated our losses this year at about five thousand dollars.
- a-zh. 我們估計今年的損失約為五千美元。
- b. The final cost of the project was not much more than the original estimate.
- b-zh. 這個專案的最終成本並沒有比原本的估價高出多少。
- 注意: 動詞唸 /-meɪt/;名詞唸 /-mət/
- 衍生: estimation 估計;underestimate 低估;overestimate 高估

## 8. impose (v.) — 強制執行、迫使

- a. The company will impose a surcharge for any items returned.
- a-zh. 公司將對任何退回的商品加收附加費。
- b. We should not impose upon our staff by requiring them to work on weekends.
- b-zh. 我們不該要求員工週末上班而打擾他們。
- 用法: impose sth on / upon sb 把⋯強加於某人
- 衍生: imposition 強加、負擔;imposing 壯觀的

## 9. mistake (n.) — 錯誤

- a. I made a mistake in adding up your bill and we overcharged you twenty dollars.
- a-zh. 我把你的帳單加錯了,多收了你二十美元。
- b. It was a mistake thinking that my boss would be reasonable when I explained my situation to him.
- b-zh. 當初我向老闆說明我的情況時,以為他會講理,那是個錯誤。
- 搭配: make a mistake 犯錯;by mistake 錯誤地
- 延伸: 當動詞時不規則:mistake–mistook–mistaken
- 衍生: mistaken 錯誤的

## 10. promptly (adv.) — 立即地、準時地

- a. We always reply promptly to customers' letters.
- a-zh. 我們總是迅速回覆客戶的來信。
- b. The new sales agent promptly offered a full refund for the damaged goods.
- b-zh. 這位新業務員立刻為受損的貨品提供全額退款。
- 同義: on time、punctually、immediately
- 衍生: prompt 迅速的;promptness 迅速

## 11. rectify (v.) — 矯正、改正

- a. He rectified the problem by giving the customer credit for the unused items that she returned.
- a-zh. 他針對客戶退回的未使用商品給予抵用金,解決了這個問題。
- b. Embarrassed at his behavior, he rectified the situation by writing a letter of apology.
- b-zh. 他對自己的行為感到難為情,於是寫了一封道歉信來彌補。
- 同義: correct、fix、set right
- 衍生: rectification 矯正;rectifiable 可糾正的

## 12. terms (n.) — 條件、條款

- a. The terms of payment were clearly listed at the bottom of the invoice.
- a-zh. 付款條件清楚列在發票的底部。
- b. The terms of the agreement required that items be fully paid for before they would be shipped.
- b-zh. 協議條款要求貨品必須付清全款才會出貨。
- 注意: 表「條件、條款」時用複數 terms
- 搭配: terms and conditions 條款與條件;terms of payment 付款條件;in terms of 就⋯而言
