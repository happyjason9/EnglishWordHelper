## 1. abide by (v.) — 遵守、順從

- a. All contractors must abide by the site safety rules.
- a-zh. 所有承包商都必須遵守工地安全規則。
- b. We agreed to abide by the outcome of the arbitration.
- b-zh. 我們同意遵守仲裁結果。
- 用法: 一定要接 by,不能說 abide the rules
- 同義: comply with、conform to、observe

## 2. agreement (n.) — 協議、合約、同意

- a. The two companies reached an agreement after months of talks.
- a-zh. 兩家公司經過數月談判後達成協議。
- b. Under the agreement, the supplier delivers within ten days.
- b-zh. 依照協議,供應商須於十天內交貨。
- 搭配: reach / sign / renew an agreement
- 片語: be in agreement with 與⋯意見一致

## 3. assurance (n.) — 保證、把握、自信

- a. He gave us his assurance that the shipment would arrive on time.
- a-zh. 他向我們保證貨物會準時抵達。
- b. She answered the client's questions with complete assurance.
- b-zh. 她非常有把握地回答客戶的問題。
- 易混: insurance 是保險,只差一個字母
- 延伸: quality assurance 品質保證(QA)

## 4. cancellation (n.) — 取消、中止

- a. The cancellation of the order cost the factory two weeks of work.
- a-zh. 訂單取消讓工廠損失兩週的產能。
- b. Please review the cancellation policy before booking.
- b-zh. 訂房前請詳閱取消政策。
- 搭配: cancellation fee / policy / clause
- 拼字: 名詞雙 l,美式動詞過去式 canceled 單 l

## 5. determine (v.) — 查明、判定、決定

- a. The audit will determine whether the fees were properly charged.
- a-zh. 稽核將查明費用是否收取得當。
- b. Market demand determines the final price.
- b-zh. 市場需求決定最終價格。
- 用法: determine whether / if + 子句
- 衍生: determination 決心;determined 有決心的

## 6. engage (v.) — 參與、使投入;聘僱

- a. The firm engaged a consultant to review the contract.
- a-zh. 公司聘請顧問審閱合約。
- b. Employees are encouraged to engage in training programs.
- b-zh. 公司鼓勵員工參與培訓課程。
- 用法: engage in sth 自己投入;engage sb. in sth 讓別人加入
- 衍生: engagement 約定、訂婚;engaging 引人入勝的

## 7. establish (v.) — 建立、確立、創辦

- a. The company was established in Taipei in 1998.
- a-zh. 該公司於 1998 年在台北成立。
- b. The contract establishes clear payment terms.
- b-zh. 這份合約確立了明確的付款條件。
- 搭配: established in + 年份;a well-established firm 老字號
- 衍生: establishment 機構、設立

## 8. obligate (v.) — 使負有法律或道義上的責任

- a. The agreement obligates both sides to share maintenance costs.
- a-zh. 該協議使雙方都有義務分攤維護費用。
- b. Employees are not obligated to work on public holidays.
- b-zh. 員工沒有義務在國定假日上班。
- 用法: 幾乎只用被動:be obligated to + 原形動詞
- 衍生: obligation 義務;have an obligation to⋯

## 9. party (n.) — 當事人、一方(非派對)

- a. Neither party may transfer the contract without written consent.
- a-zh. 未經書面同意,任何一方不得轉讓合約。
- b. Disputes will be settled by a neutral third party.
- b-zh. 爭議將由中立的第三方解決。
- 搭配: both parties 雙方;the parties concerned 相關當事人
- 延伸: third-party vendor 第三方廠商

## 10. provision (n.) — 條款、規定;事先的準備

- a. The contract includes a provision for early termination.
- a-zh. 合約中包含提前終止的條款。
- b. Under this provision, either side may renegotiate the price.
- b-zh. 依本條款,任一方均可重新議價。
- 同義: clause、stipulation、term
- 注意: 複數 provisions 另有「糧食補給」之意

## 11. resolve (v.) — 解決;下定決心

- a. The two sides resolved the dispute without going to court.
- a-zh. 雙方未經訴訟就解決了爭議。
- b. She resolved to renegotiate the terms next quarter.
- b-zh. 她決心下一季重新談判條件。
- 用法: resolve a problem 解決;resolve to V 決心
- 衍生: resolution 決議、解決方案

## 12. specific (adj.) — 特定的、明確具體的

- a. Please be specific about the delivery date.
- a-zh. 請明確說明交貨日期。
- b. The manual gives specific instructions for installation.
- b-zh. 手冊提供了具體的安裝說明。
- 反義: general、vague
- 衍生: specify 指明;specification 規格;specifically 明確地
