## 1. desire (v.) — 渴望

- a. We desire to have our own home.
- a-zh. 我們渴望擁有自己的房子。
- b. He desires to retire when he becomes forty.
- b-zh. 他希望四十歲就退休。
- 用法: desire to V 渴望做某事
- 衍生: desirable 令人嚮往的;desired 想要的

## 2. detail (v.) — 詳述

- a. The business planner detailed the steps we should follow to write our financial statement.
- a-zh. 商業規劃師詳細說明了我們撰寫財務報表應遵循的步驟。
- b. Fabio created a financial statement that detailed every expected expenditure for the next quarter.
- b-zh. 法比歐製作了一份財務報表,詳列下一季每一筆預期支出。
- 用法: 名詞為「細節」;in detail 詳細地
- 衍生: detailed 詳細的

## 3. forecast (n./v.) — 預報,預測

- a. The financial forecast indicates a deficit in the next quarter.
- a-zh. 財務預測顯示下一季將出現赤字。
- b. Analysts forecast a strong economic outlook.
- b-zh. 分析師預測經濟前景強勁。
- 拼字: 過去式可用 forecast 或 forecasted
- 搭配: weather forecast 天氣預報;sales forecast 銷售預測

## 4. level (n.) — 等級

- a. We have never had an accountant work at such a sophisticated level before.
- a-zh. 我們以前從沒請過水準這麼高的會計師。
- b. If you expect your business to earn at higher levels, your financial statement should show that.
- b-zh. 如果你預期你的事業獲利會更高,財務報表就應該呈現出來。
- 搭配: at a high level 在高水準;level of income 收入水準

## 5. overall (adj.) — 全部的,整體的

- a. The company's overall expectations were out of proportion.
- a-zh. 這家公司的整體期望太不切實際了。
- b. Our overall financial situation is not too bad.
- b-zh. 我們整體的財務狀況還不算太糟。
- 用法: 也可作副詞「整體而言」,如 Overall, it went well.
- 片語: out of proportion 不成比例、過度

## 6. perspective (n.) — 觀點,看法

- a. The budget statement gave us perspective on where the costs of running the business are found.
- a-zh. 預算表讓我們看清經營這門生意的成本出在哪裡。
- b. Joseph's accountant gave him some perspective as well as some data on how much he could expect to earn in his first year in business.
- b-zh. 約瑟夫的會計師給了他一些看法,也提供了一些數據,說明他創業第一年預計能賺多少。
- 搭配: put sth in perspective 正確看待某事;from my perspective 從我的觀點
- 同義: viewpoint、outlook

## 7. project (v.) — 預估,推測

- a. We need to project our earnings and expenses in order to plan next year's budget.
- a-zh. 我們需要預估收入與支出,才能規劃明年的預算。
- b. The director projects that the company will need to hire ten new employees this year.
- b-zh. 主管預估公司今年需要雇用十名新員工。
- 發音: 動詞重音在後 pro-JECT;名詞「專案」重音在前 PRO-ject
- 衍生: projection 預測

## 8. realistic (adj.) — 現實的,實際可行的

- a. An accurate accounting gave Stefano a realistic idea of his business's financial direction.
- a-zh. 準確的帳目讓史蒂法諾對自己事業的財務走向有了實際的認識。
- b. Realistic expectations are important when you review your financial statements.
- b-zh. 檢視財務報表時,抱持實際的期望很重要。
- 反義: unrealistic 不切實際的
- 衍生: reality 現實;realistically 實際地

## 9. target (v./n.) — 把⋯作為目標;目標

- a. We targeted March as the deadline for completing the financial statement.
- a-zh. 我們把三月訂為完成財務報表的期限。
- b. Matilde hoped to sign on five new clients last month, but she didn't reach her target.
- b-zh. 瑪蒂妲原本希望上個月簽下五位新客戶,但她沒有達成目標。
- 搭配: reach / meet a target 達成目標;target market 目標市場
- 拼字: targeted 不重複子音(重音在前)

## 10. translation (n.) — 翻譯

- a. The translation of the statement from Japanese into English was very helpful.
- a-zh. 把報表從日文譯成英文非常有幫助。
- b. The accountant provided a translation of the financial terms in a language we could all understand.
- b-zh. 會計師把財務術語翻譯成我們都聽得懂的語言。
- 衍生: translate 翻譯;translator 譯者
- 用法: translate A into B 把 A 翻成 B

## 11. typically (adv.) — 典型地

- a. Office expenses typically include such things as salaries, rent, and office supplies.
- a-zh. 辦公室開支通常包括薪資、租金和辦公用品等項目。
- b. Banks typically require a financial statement before they will lend money to a small business.
- b-zh. 銀行通常會先要求財務報表,才肯借錢給小型企業。
- 同義: usually、generally
- 衍生: typical 典型的

## 12. yield (n./v.) — 產量;產生

- a. Henry's budget gave him the desired yield: a better indication of his expected profit.
- a-zh. 亨利的預算帶給他想要的成果:更清楚地掌握預期利潤。
- b. The company's investment yielded high returns.
- b-zh. 這家公司的投資帶來了高報酬。
- 多義: 另有「讓步、屈服」之意;路標 YIELD 為「讓行」
- 搭配: yield a profit / return 產生利潤、報酬
