## 1. characteristic (adj./n.) — 特有的、典型的;特徵

- a. It is characteristic of this brand to offer only a one-year warranty.
- a-zh. 只提供一年保固是這個品牌的典型作法。
- b. Slow customer response is an unfortunate characteristic of that company.
- b-zh. 回應顧客速度慢是那家公司一個令人詬病的特徵。
- 用法: be characteristic of + 對象;當名詞常用複數 characteristics(諸多特徵)
- 衍生: character 性格、特質;characterize 以⋯為特徵

## 2. consequence (n.) — 結果、後果

- a. Skipping the annual inspection may void the warranty as a consequence.
- a-zh. 沒做年度檢查的後果可能是保固失效。
- b. As a consequence of the delay, the extended warranty period was shortened.
- b-zh. 由於延遲的緣故,延長保固的期間被縮短了。
- 片語: as a consequence (of) 因此、由於⋯的結果
- 易混: 常與 consequently(因此,連接詞用法)混用,注意詞性

## 3. consider (v.) — 考慮

- a. Consider the warranty terms carefully before signing the contract.
- a-zh. 簽約前請仔細考慮保固條款。
- b. After considering several brands, she chose the one with the longest warranty.
- b-zh. 比較過幾個品牌後,她選了保固最長的那個。
- 用法: consider + V-ing(不接 to V)
- 衍生: consideration 考慮;considerable 相當大的

## 4. cover (v.) — 涵蓋、給⋯保障

- a. The warranty covers parts and labor for the first two years.
- a-zh. 保固涵蓋前兩年的零件與工資。
- b. This policy does not cover damage caused by misuse.
- b-zh. 此保單不涵蓋因誤用造成的損壞。
- 衍生: coverage 涵蓋範圍、保障範圍(常考名詞)

## 5. expire (v.) — 終止、到期

- a. The manufacturer's warranty expires after twelve months.
- a-zh. 製造商的保固在十二個月後到期。
- b. Once the warranty expires, repair costs are the owner's responsibility.
- b-zh. 保固一旦到期,維修費用就由物主自行負擔。
- 衍生: expiration 到期、屆滿;expiration date 有效期限

## 6. frequently (adv.) — 經常地

- a. Customers frequently ask whether accidental damage is covered.
- a-zh. 顧客經常詢問意外損壞是否有保固。
- b. Extended warranties are frequently sold at a discount during promotions.
- b-zh. 促銷期間延長保固經常有折扣。
- 衍生: frequent (adj.) 頻繁的;frequency 頻率

## 7. imply (v.) — 指示、暗示(以推論方式)

- a. The advertisement implied that all repairs would be free of charge.
- a-zh. 廣告暗示所有維修都會是免費的。
- b. A long warranty period often implies confidence in product quality.
- b-zh. 保固期長通常暗示著對產品品質的信心。
- 易混: imply(說話者暗示) vs. infer(聽者推論),方向相反,常考。
- 衍生: implication 暗示、含意

## 8. promise (n./v.) — 承諾;保證、承諾提供

- a. A warranty is essentially a promise to fix or replace a defective product.
- a-zh. 保固本質上就是承諾維修或更換瑕疵產品。
- b. The company promised full refunds for any product returned within 30 days.
- b-zh. 公司承諾三十天內退貨可獲全額退款。
- 用法: promise to V;promise sb. that 子句

## 9. protect (v.) — 保護

- a. This warranty protects buyers against manufacturing defects.
- a-zh. 此保固保護買家免受製造瑕疵之害。
- b. Keeping the receipt protects you if you need to make a claim.
- b-zh. 保留收據可以在你需要理賠時保護自己。
- 片語: protect A against/from B
- 衍生: protection 保護;protective 具保護性的

## 10. reputation (n.) — 聲譽

- a. The brand's reputation for reliability justifies its higher price.
- a-zh. 該品牌可靠的聲譽足以說明其較高的價格。
- b. A single bad review can damage a company's reputation quickly.
- b-zh. 一則負評就能迅速損害公司的聲譽。
- 片語: have a reputation for + N/V-ing;damage/build a reputation

## 11. require (v.) — 要求、需要

- a. The warranty requires proof of purchase for any claim.
- a-zh. 此保固要求理賠時須提供購買證明。
- b. Some warranties require the product to be registered within 30 days.
- b-zh. 有些保固要求產品須在三十天內完成登記。
- 用法: require sb. to V;require that + 子句(常用原形動詞,虛擬語氣)
- 衍生: requirement 要求、條件

## 12. variety (n.) — 各式各樣、多樣性

- a. The store offers a variety of extended warranty plans.
- a-zh. 這家店提供多種延長保固方案。
- b. Warranty terms vary widely across a variety of product categories.
- b-zh. 保固條款在各類產品之間差異很大。
- 片語: a variety of + 複數名詞(視為複數)
- 衍生: vary 使多樣化、變化;various 各式各樣的
