## 1. address (n./v.) — 演說、地址;提出、處理

- a. The board praised her address at the annual shareholders' meeting.
- a-zh. 董事會讚賞她在年度股東會上的演說。
- b. Our business plan directly addresses the needs of first-time buyers.
- b-zh. 我們的商業計畫直接回應了首次購買者的需求。
- 用法: 動詞 address a problem/issue/need,即「處理、正視」某個問題,是商業計畫常考用法

## 2. avoid (v.) — 避免

- a. To avoid cash-flow problems, the owner drew up a detailed budget.
- a-zh. 為了避免現金流問題,老闆擬定了詳細的預算。
- b. Many of these mistakes could have been avoided with a proper business plan.
- b-zh. 這些錯誤大多本可靠著完善的商業計畫來避免。
- 用法: avoid + V-ing(不接 to V)

## 3. demonstrate (v.) — 論證、說明、展現

- a. The plan demonstrates a clear understanding of the target market.
- a-zh. 這份計畫展現出對目標市場的清楚了解。
- b. She demonstrated with sales figures that the strategy was working.
- b-zh. 她用銷售數字證明這項策略是有效的。
- 衍生: demonstration 展示、說明

## 4. develop (v.) — 發展、擴展

- a. He developed his idea into a full business plan over six months.
- a-zh. 他花了六個月把構想發展成完整的商業計畫。
- b. The small shop has developed into a chain with twenty locations.
- b-zh. 這家小店已發展成擁有二十間分店的連鎖店。
- 衍生: development 發展;developer 開發者

## 5. evaluate (v.) — 評價、評估

- a. Before expanding, the team evaluated the risks of entering a new market.
- a-zh. 在擴張前,團隊評估了進入新市場的風險。
- b. Lenders evaluate a company's credit history before approving a loan.
- b-zh. 放款方在核准貸款前會評估公司的信用紀錄。
- 衍生: evaluation 評估、評價

## 6. gather (v.) — 收集、召集;推測

- a. We gathered market data from surveys, interviews, and public reports.
- a-zh. 我們透過問卷、訪談和公開報告收集市場資料。
- b. I gather that the new regulations will affect small businesses the most.
- b-zh. 我推測新法規對小型企業影響最大。
- 用法: I gather (that) 表示「我推測、聽說」,常出現在口語或報告開頭

## 7. offer (n./v.) — 出價、提議;提供、主動提出

- a. She accepted the investor's offer to fund the second phase.
- a-zh. 她接受了投資人資助第二階段的提議。
- b. The plan must offer lenders solid evidence of future profitability.
- b-zh. 這份計畫必須向放款方提出未來獲利的具體證據。
- 用法: offer sb. sth;offer to V

## 8. primarily (adv.) — 首要地、最重要地

- a. The proposal is primarily concerned with reducing operating costs.
- a-zh. 這份提案主要著重在降低營運成本。
- b. Investors are thinking primarily about long-term returns.
- b-zh. 投資人主要考慮的是長期報酬。
- 衍生: primary (adj.) 主要的、首要的

## 9. risk (n.) — 風險

- a. The biggest risk for new businesses is running out of capital too early.
- a-zh. 新創企業最大的風險是太早耗盡資金。
- b. Entering an unfamiliar market always carries some risk.
- b-zh. 進入陌生市場總是帶有一定的風險。
- 片語: at risk 處於風險中;take a risk 冒險

## 10. strategy (n.) — 策略

- a. A solid business plan lays out a clear strategy for growth.
- a-zh. 完善的商業計畫會列出明確的成長策略。
- b. The team is developing a strategy to enter the online market.
- b-zh. 團隊正在擬定進軍網路市場的策略。
- 衍生: strategic 策略性的;strategist 策略家

## 11. strengthen (v.) — 加強、使更強

- a. Including market research can strengthen your business plan.
- a-zh. 加入市場研究可以強化你的商業計畫。
- b. The company strengthened its position by acquiring a smaller competitor.
- b-zh. 該公司透過併購一家較小的競爭對手來鞏固地位。
- 反義: weaken(削弱)

## 12. substitution (n.) — 代替、替換

- a. The substitution of low-cost materials raised concerns about quality.
- a-zh. 以低成本材料替換的做法引發了品質疑慮。
- b. There is no substitution for thorough market research.
- b-zh. 徹底的市場研究是無可取代的。
- 衍生: substitute (v./n.) 替代、代替品
