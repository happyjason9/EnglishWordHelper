## 1. accept (v.) — 接受

- a. He could not manage his bank account online because the website would not accept his password.
- a-zh. 他無法在網路上管理銀行帳戶,因為網站不接受他的密碼。
- b. Without hesitating, she accepted the job of teller.
- b-zh. 她毫不猶豫地接下了銀行櫃員的工作。
- 衍生: acceptance 接受;acceptable 可接受的
- 易混: accept 接受;except 除了

## 2. balance (n./v.) — 餘額;收支平衡

- a. His healthy bank balance showed a long habit of savings.
- a-zh. 他可觀的銀行存款餘額,顯示他長期有儲蓄的習慣。
- b. It took him over an hour to balance his checkbook.
- b-zh. 他花了一個多小時才把支票簿的收支對平。
- 搭配: bank balance 銀行餘額;balance a checkbook 核對支票簿收支
- 延伸: 另有「平衡」之意,如 work-life balance

## 3. borrow (v.) — 借入

- a. Myra borrowed a pen so that she could sign the check.
- a-zh. 麥拉借了一支筆,好在支票上簽名。
- b. The couple borrowed money from the bank to buy a home.
- b-zh. 這對夫妻向銀行借錢買房子。
- 用法: borrow sth from sb 向某人借某物
- 易混: borrow 借入;lend 借出

## 4. cautiously (adv.) — 小心地

- a. The bank manager spoke cautiously when giving out information to people she did not know.
- a-zh. 銀行經理對不認識的人提供資訊時,說話很謹慎。
- b. Act cautiously when signing contracts and read them thoroughly first.
- b-zh. 簽約時要謹慎行事,先把合約徹底讀過。
- 同義: carefully、warily
- 衍生: cautious 小心的;caution 小心、警告

## 5. deduct (v.) — 扣除,減除

- a. If you choose, the bank will deduct regular charges, such as electric bills, from your account.
- a-zh. 如果你願意,銀行會從你的帳戶中扣繳電費之類的固定費用。
- b. By deducting the monthly fee from her checking account, Yi was able to make her account balance.
- b-zh. 從支票帳戶中扣掉月費後,小怡的帳目就對平了。
- 用法: deduct A from B 從 B 中扣除 A
- 衍生: deduction 扣除額;deductible 可扣除的
- 同義: subtract

## 6. dividend (n.) — 紅利,股息

- a. The stockholders were outraged when their quarterly dividends were so small.
- a-zh. 股東們看到季度股息這麼少,都非常憤怒。
- b. The dividend was calculated and distributed to the group.
- b-zh. 紅利已經計算好並分配給這群人。
- 搭配: pay a dividend 發放股息
- 延伸: pay dividends 帶來好處、有回報

## 7. down payment (n.) — 頭期款

- a. Mary bought her car with an initial down payment in cash and then made her monthly payments online.
- a-zh. 瑪莉買車時先用現金付了頭期款,之後每月在網路上繳分期款。
- b. Karl was disappointed when the real estate agent told him he needed a larger down payment on the house.
- b-zh. 房仲告訴卡爾買這棟房子需要付更多頭期款時,他很失望。
- 用法: a down payment on sth 某物的頭期款
- 搭配: make a down payment 付頭期款;monthly payment 月付款

## 8. mortgage (n./v.) — 抵押(借款);抵押借款

- a. Due to low interest rates, Sheila moved quickly to find a good deal on a mortgage.
- a-zh. 由於利率很低,希拉趕緊去找條件好的房貸。
- b. Hiram mortgaged his home to get extra money to invest in his business.
- b-zh. 海勒姆把房子拿去抵押,好籌到額外資金投入他的事業。
- 發音: t 不發音 /ˈmɔːrɡɪdʒ/
- 搭配: take out a mortgage 申辦房貸;pay off a mortgage 還清房貸

## 9. restricted (adj.) — 受限的

- a. The number of free withdrawals a customer can make each month is restricted to five.
- a-zh. 顧客每個月可免費提款的次數限制為五次。
- b. Access to the safe deposit box vault is restricted to key holders.
- b-zh. 保險箱金庫只限持有鑰匙的人進入。
- 用法: be restricted to sth 僅限於⋯
- 衍生: restrict 限制;restriction 限制
- 同義: limited

## 10. signature (n.) — 簽名

- a. Once we have your signature, the contract will be complete.
- a-zh. 一旦有了你的簽名,這份合約就完成了。
- b. The customer's signature was kept on file for identification purposes.
- b-zh. 為了身分辨識,客戶的簽名留存在檔案中。
- 衍生: sign 簽名(v.)
- 易混: 名人簽名是 autograph

## 11. take out (v. 片語) — 提領(金錢),拿走

- a. My checking account allows me to take out money at any bank branch without a fee.
- a-zh. 我的支票帳戶讓我可以在任何一家分行免手續費提款。
- b. They took out the chairs in the bank lobby so now there is no place to sit.
- b-zh. 他們把銀行大廳的椅子搬走了,所以現在沒地方坐。
- b-cloze: took out
- 同義: withdraw
- 搭配: take out a loan 貸款

## 12. transaction (n.) — 交易

- a. Banking transactions will appear on your monthly statement.
- a-zh. 銀行交易會列在你的月結對帳單上。
- b. The most common transactions can be made from your personal computer.
- b-zh. 最常見的交易都可以用你的個人電腦辦理。
- 衍生: transact 辦理、交易
- 搭配: make a transaction 進行交易
