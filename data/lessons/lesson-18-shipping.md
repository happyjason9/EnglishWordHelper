## 1. accurately (adv.) — 正確地

- a. To gauge these figures accurately, we first need to get some facts from the shipping department.
- a-zh. 要準確評估這些數字,我們首先需要從出貨部門取得一些事實資料。
- b. The container company must balance the load accurately or there could be a disaster at sea.
- b-zh. 貨櫃公司必須準確平衡載重,否則海上可能發生災難。
- 同義: correctly、precisely
- 衍生: accurate 準確的;accuracy 準確度;inaccurate 不準確的

## 2. carrier (n.) — 運送人

- a. Lou, our favorite carrier, takes extra care of our boxes marked "fragile."
- a-zh. 我們最愛的運送員盧,對標示「易碎」的箱子格外小心。
- b. Mr. Lau switched carriers in order to get a price savings on deliveries out of state.
- b-zh. 劉先生換了貨運公司,好省下寄往外州貨件的運費。
- 搭配: air carrier 航空公司;mail carrier 郵差
- 衍生: carry 運送、攜帶

## 3. catalog (n./v.) — 目錄;編入目錄

- a. The fall catalog shows a number of items that Mr. Lau has never before been able to offer.
- a-zh. 秋季目錄中有幾項是劉先生以前從未能提供的商品。
- b. Ellen cataloged the complaints according to severity.
- b-zh. 艾倫依照嚴重程度將客訴歸類整理。
- 拼字: 美式 catalog;英式 catalogue
- 用法: 名詞動詞同形;動詞變化 cataloged

## 4. fulfill (v.) — 完成、履行、達到(目的)

- a. All orders are fulfilled and ready for shipment within twenty-four hours.
- a-zh. 所有訂單都會在二十四小時內完成處理並準備出貨。
- b. Her expectations were so high, we knew they would be hard to fulfill.
- b-zh. 她的期望太高,我們知道會很難達到。
- 拼字: 美式 fulfill;英式 fulfil;過去式 fulfilled
- 搭配: fulfill an order / a requirement / a promise 履行訂單、要求、承諾
- 衍生: fulfillment 履行、滿足

## 5. integral (adj.) — 構成整體不可缺的

- a. Good customer relations are an integral component of any business.
- a-zh. 良好的客戶關係是任何企業不可或缺的組成部分。
- b. A dependable stream of inventory is integral to reliable shipping of orders.
- b-zh. 穩定的存貨供應流對訂單的可靠出貨是不可或缺的。
- 用法: be integral to sth 是⋯不可缺的
- 同義: essential、indispensable
- 衍生: integrate 整合;integration 整合

## 6. inventory (n.) — 存貨(清單)

- a. The store closes one day a year so that the staff can take inventory of the stockroom.
- a-zh. 這家店一年停業一天,讓員工能盤點庫房的存貨。
- b. Their inventory has not changed much over the years.
- b-zh. 多年來他們的存貨並沒有太大變化。
- 搭配: take inventory 盤點;in inventory 有庫存
- 同義: stock

## 7. minimize (v.) — 減到最少、降低

- a. The shipping staff minimized complaints by working overtime to deliver the packages quickly.
- a-zh. 出貨人員加班迅速送出包裹,把客訴降到最低。
- b. To keep customers happy and minimize the effect of the strike, we shipped orders directly to them.
- b-zh. 為了讓客戶滿意並把罷工的影響降到最低,我們直接把訂單寄給他們。
- 反義: maximize 使最大化
- 衍生: minimum 最小值;minimal 最小的
- 拼字: 英式 minimise

## 8. on hand (adj.) — 在手邊、在場

- a. We had too much stock on hand, so we had a summer sale.
- a-zh. 我們手邊庫存太多,所以辦了夏季大特賣。
- b. The new employee will be on hand if we need more help with shipping orders.
- b-zh. 如果出貨作業需要更多人手,這位新員工會隨時待命。
- 同義: available、in stock
- 易混: on hand 是手邊有;on the other hand 是「另一方面」

## 9. receive (v.) — 收到

- a. You will receive your order within one week of the shipping date.
- a-zh. 你會在出貨日起一週內收到訂單。
- b. When we received the package, the box was broken and some of the contents were missing.
- b-zh. 我們收到包裹時,箱子已經破了,部分內容物不見了。
- 拼字: i 在 e 之前,但 c 之後相反:receive
- 衍生: receipt 收據;recipient 收件人;reception 接待

## 10. ship (v.) — 運送

- a. We shipped the package yesterday, so it should arrive by the end of the week.
- a-zh. 我們昨天寄出了包裹,所以應該會在本週末前送達。
- b. Very few customers think about how their packages will be shipped, and are seldom home when the packages arrive.
- b-zh. 很少有顧客會去想自己的包裹是怎麼運送的,而且包裹送達時他們很少在家。
- 拼字: 重複子音:shipped、shipping
- 衍生: shipment 貨運;shipper 託運人
- 搭配: ship an order 出貨

## 11. sufficiently (adv.) — 充足地

- a. Customers aren't choosing express shipping because it hasn't been sufficiently advertised.
- a-zh. 顧客沒有選擇快遞運送,是因為宣傳得不夠充分。
- b. We are sufficiently organized to begin transferring the palettes tomorrow.
- b-zh. 我們已有充分的安排,明天就能開始搬運貨板。
- 同義: enough、adequately
- 衍生: sufficient 足夠的;suffice 足夠;insufficient 不足的

## 12. supply (n./v.) — 供給、補給品;供應

- a. By making better use of our supplies, we can avoid ordering until next month.
- a-zh. 只要更善用我們的備品,就能避免在下個月之前訂貨。
- b. Gerald supplied the shipping staff with enough labels to last a year.
- b-zh. 傑拉德供給出貨人員足夠使用一年的標籤。
- 搭配: supply and demand 供需;in short supply 供應短缺
- 用法: supply sb with sth 供應某人某物
- 衍生: supplier 供應商
