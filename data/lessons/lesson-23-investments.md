## 1. aggressively (adv.) — 好競爭地,武斷地

- a. Some people are risk takers and prefer to invest aggressively.
- a-zh. 有些人喜歡冒險,偏好積極進取地投資。
- b. His ideas were not well received because he spoke so aggressively.
- b-zh. 他的想法不受歡迎,因為他說話太咄咄逼人。
- 衍生: aggressive 積極的、具侵略性的;aggression 侵略
- 反義: conservatively 保守地

## 2. attitude (n.) — 態度

- a. The new fund manager's attitude changed quickly after the first big downturn in the market.
- a-zh. 市場第一次大幅下跌後,新任基金經理人的態度很快就變了。
- b. Each investor should assess his or her own attitude toward investment.
- b-zh. 每位投資人都應評估自己對投資的態度。
- 用法: attitude toward / to sth 對⋯的態度
- 易混: aptitude 天資;altitude 高度

## 3. commit (v.) — 投入,做出保證

- a. It is a good idea to commit a certain percentage of your income to investments.
- a-zh. 把收入的一定比例投入投資,是個好主意。
- b. The stockbroker committed herself to finding the best investments for each client.
- b-zh. 這位股票經紀人致力於為每位客戶找到最好的投資標的。
- 用法: commit A to B 把 A 投入 B;commit oneself to V-ing 致力於(to 是介係詞)
- 拼字: 重複子音:committed、committing
- 衍生: commitment 承諾、投入

## 4. conservative (adj.) — 保守的,謹慎的

- a. Her conservative strategy paid off over the years.
- a-zh. 她保守的策略多年下來獲得了回報。
- b. Generally, older people should be more conservative in their investing than younger people.
- b-zh. 一般來說,年長者的投資應該比年輕人更保守。
- 衍生: conserve 保存;conservation 保育
- 反義: aggressive 積極的

## 5. fund (n.) — 資金,基金

- a. If you have extra funds, talk to your stockbroker about the best place to put them.
- a-zh. 如果你有多餘的資金,可以和你的股票經紀人談談該投在哪裡最好。
- b. My stockbroker recommended investing in this fund.
- b-zh. 我的股票經紀人推薦投資這檔基金。
- 搭配: mutual fund 共同基金;raise funds 募款
- 易混: fund 資金;refund 退款

## 6. invest (v.) — 投資

- a. The chief financial officer invested in the stock at a very good time.
- a-zh. 財務長在非常好的時機買進了這檔股票。
- b. Don't invest all of your time in just one project.
- b-zh. 別把你所有的時間都投入在單一專案上。
- 用法: invest (money / time) in sth 把(錢、時間)投入某事
- 衍生: investment 投資;investor 投資人

## 7. long-term (adj.) — 長期的

- a. The CEO's long-term goal was to increase the return on investment.
- a-zh. 執行長的長期目標是提高投資報酬率。
- b. Long-term investments are not really affected by the daily ups and downs of the stock market.
- b-zh. 長期投資其實不太受股市每日漲跌的影響。
- 反義: short-term 短期的
- 搭配: in the long term 從長遠來看

## 8. portfolio (n.) — 投資組合

- a. Investors are advised to have diverse portfolios.
- a-zh. 投資人被建議要有多元化的投資組合。
- b. The investor's portfolio consisted of blue chip company stocks and government bonds.
- b-zh. 這位投資人的投資組合包含績優股和政府公債。
- 延伸: 另指「作品集」;diverse 多樣的
- 搭配: blue chip 績優股;government bond 政府公債

## 9. pull out (v. 片語) — 撤出,退出

- a. A lot of people pulled out their money when it became clear that the bank was in trouble.
- a-zh. 當銀行明顯陷入困境時,很多人把錢撤了出來。
- a-cloze: pulled out
- b. He pulled out of the company and took all of his money with him.
- b-zh. 他退出了公司,並帶走了他所有的錢。
- b-cloze: pulled out
- 用法: pull out of sth 退出某事
- 同義: withdraw

## 10. resource (n.) — 資產,資源

- a. If you don't invest in petroleum resources now, you will see the stock prices get away from you.
- a-zh. 如果你現在不投資石油資源,就會眼看著股價漲到你追不上。
- b. The company's most valuable resource was its staff.
- b-zh. 這家公司最寶貴的資源是它的員工。
- 搭配: human resources 人力資源;natural resources 天然資源
- 衍生: resourceful 足智多謀的

## 11. return (n.) — 利潤

- a. The 44 percent return on the new stock was far more than the stockbroker had anticipated.
- a-zh. 新股票百分之四十四的報酬,遠遠超出股票經紀人的預期。
- b. Some investors are satisfied with a 5% return, while others want a much larger return.
- b-zh. 有些投資人滿足於百分之五的報酬,有些人則想要高得多的報酬。
- 多義: 動詞「返回、歸還、回覆」
- 搭配: return on investment (ROI) 投資報酬率

## 12. wisely (adv.) — 明智地,聰穎地

- a. If you invest wisely, you will be able to retire early.
- a-zh. 如果你投資得明智,就能提早退休。
- b. Mary wisely followed her stockbroker's advice and took her money out of some bad investments.
- b-zh. 瑪莉明智地聽從了股票經紀人的建議,把錢從一些不好的投資中撤出。
- 衍生: wise 明智的;wisdom 智慧
