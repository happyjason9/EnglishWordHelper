## 1. accommodate (v.) — 容納;提供(住宿、需要的東西)

- a. The conference center can accommodate up to 500 attendees.
- a-zh. 這個會議中心最多可容納 500 位與會者。
- b. The hotel was fully booked and could not accommodate our late request.
- b-zh. 飯店已經客滿,無法容納我們臨時的需求。
- 衍生: accommodation(s) 住宿設施

## 2. arrangement (n.) — 安排、整理

- a. All travel arrangements for the speakers were handled by the events team.
- a-zh. 講者的所有交通安排都由活動團隊處理。
- b. The seating arrangement was designed to encourage networking.
- b-zh. 座位安排的設計是為了促進人脈交流。
- 片語: make arrangements for 為⋯做安排

## 3. association (n.) — 協會、聯盟;關係、聯想

- a. Membership in the trade association gives access to exclusive events.
- a-zh. 加入該貿易協會可以參加專屬活動。
- b. Her long association with the organizing committee made the process smooth.
- b-zh. 她與籌辦委員會長期的合作關係讓流程進行得很順利。
- 易混: 課本標注「聯想」是另一常見意思,如 in association with 與⋯合作

## 4. attend (v.) — 參加;注意、照料

- a. More than 300 delegates are expected to attend the opening session.
- a-zh. 預計超過三百位代表會參加開幕場次。
- b. Staff attended to every request from VIP guests.
- b-zh. 工作人員照料了貴賓的每一項需求。
- 用法: attend a meeting(參加,及物);attend to sb./sth.(照料,不及物)

## 5. get in touch (v. 片語) — 保持聯絡、聯繫

- a. Please get in touch with the front desk if you need extra chairs.
- a-zh. 如果需要額外的椅子,請聯繫櫃檯。
- b. Attendees can get in touch with speakers during the networking break.
- b-zh. 與會者可以在交流時段與講者聯繫。
- 用法: get in touch with sb.

## 6. hold (v.) — 容納;舉行

- a. The main hall holds up to 1,000 people.
- a-zh. 主廳最多可容納一千人。
- b. The association holds this conference every spring.
- b-zh. 該協會每年春天舉辦這場會議。
- 片語: hold a meeting/conference/seminar 舉行會議

## 7. location (n.) — 地點、位置

- a. The location of next year's conference has not yet been announced.
- a-zh. 明年會議的地點尚未公布。
- b. A downtown location makes it easier for attendees to reach by public transit.
- b-zh. 市中心的地點讓與會者更容易搭乘大眾運輸抵達。

## 8. overcrowded (adj.) — 過於擁擠的

- a. The lobby became overcrowded during the morning registration period.
- a-zh. 早上報到時段大廳變得過於擁擠。
- b. Organizers added a second session to avoid an overcrowded room.
- b-zh. 主辦單位加開第二場以避免場地過於擁擠。
- 衍生: crowd(v./n.) 群眾、擠滿;crowded 擁擠的

## 9. register (n./v.) — 登記、名冊;登記、註冊

- a. According to the register, over 400 people signed in for the keynote.
- a-zh. 根據登記名冊,有超過四百人為主題演講簽到。
- b. All attendees must register online before the conference date.
- b-zh. 所有與會者必須在會議日期前完成線上登記。
- 衍生: registration 登記、報名

## 10. select (v./adj.) — 選擇;精選的

- a. Participants can select up to three workshops to attend.
- a-zh. 參加者最多可以選擇三場工作坊參加。
- b. Only a select group of members were invited to the VIP dinner.
- b-zh. 只有一小群精選的會員受邀參加貴賓晚宴。
- 衍生: selection 選擇、精選;selective 挑剔的、有選擇性的

## 11. session (n.) — 會議、場次

- a. The afternoon session covers digital marketing strategies.
- a-zh. 下午場次涵蓋數位行銷策略。
- b. Due to high demand, the workshop will be offered in two sessions.
- b-zh. 由於需求量大,這場工作坊將分兩個場次舉行。

## 12. take part in (v. 片語) — 參加、參與

- a. Over 100 companies took part in this year's trade conference.
- a-zh. 今年有超過一百家公司參與這場貿易會議。
- a-cloze: took part in
- b. Attendees are encouraged to take part in the panel discussion.
- b-zh. 鼓勵與會者參與座談討論。
- 同義: participate in、join in
