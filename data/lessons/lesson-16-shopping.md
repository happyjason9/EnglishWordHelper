## 1. bargain (v./n.) — 討價還價;減價品

- a. She bargained for over an hour, finally reducing the price by half.
- a-zh. 她討價還價了一個多小時,最後把價格砍了一半。
- b. Lois compared the sweaters carefully to determine which was a better bargain.
- b-zh. 蘿絲仔細比較這些毛衣,判斷哪一件比較划算。
- 搭配: bargain for / over sth 為⋯討價還價;a good bargain 划算的交易
- 衍生: bargaining 議價;bargain hunter 愛撿便宜的人

## 2. bear (v.) — 忍受

- a. Moya can't bear crowded stores so she does most of her shopping online.
- a-zh. 莫雅受不了擁擠的商店,所以她大部分的購物都在網路上完成。
- b. If you can bear with me, I'd like to stop in one more store.
- b-zh. 如果你能再忍耐我一下,我想再逛一家店。
- 注意: 不規則變化 bear–bore–borne
- 用法: bear with sb 容忍某人、耐心等候
- 延伸: 另有承擔、生育、熊等意思

## 3. behavior (n.) — 行為

- a. Annu is conducting a survey on whether consumer behavior differs between men and women.
- a-zh. 安努正在進行一項調查,研究男性與女性的消費行為是否有所不同。
- b. Suspicious behavior in a department store will draw the attention of the security guards.
- b-zh. 在百貨公司裡可疑的行為會引起保全人員的注意。
- 拼字: 美式 behavior;英式 behaviour
- 衍生: behave 表現;behavioral 行為的

## 4. checkout (n.) — (超市等)結帳處;(旅館等)結帳離開的時間

- a. The line at this checkout is too long, so let's look for another.
- a-zh. 這個結帳櫃台排隊的人太多了,我們找另一個吧。
- b. This store has plenty of cashiers and checkout takes no time at all.
- b-zh. 這家店有很多收銀員,結帳一點都不費時間。
- 拼字: 名詞 checkout 寫在一起;動詞 check out 分開
- 搭配: checkout line 結帳隊伍;late checkout 延後退房

## 5. comfort (v./n.) — 安慰;舒適感

- a. Some people use shopping as a way to comfort themselves after a stressful day.
- a-zh. 有些人把購物當成壓力大的一天之後的自我安慰。
- b. I like to dress for comfort if I'm spending the day shopping.
- b-zh. 如果要花一整天逛街,我喜歡穿得舒服一點。
- 搭配: dress for comfort 穿著求舒適
- 衍生: comfortable 舒適的;uncomfortable 不舒服的

## 6. expand (v.) — 擴張、膨脹

- a. The new manager has significantly expanded the store's inventory.
- a-zh. 新經理大幅增加了店內的庫存量。
- b. The shoe store is out of room, so the owners are expanding into the adjacent vacant building.
- b-zh. 這家鞋店已經沒有空間了,所以店主正往隔壁的空建築擴張。
- 用法: expand into sth 擴展到⋯
- 衍生: expansion 擴張;expandable 可擴充的
- 反義: contract 縮小、收縮

## 7. explore (v.) — 探索

- a. The collector likes to explore antique shops looking for bargains.
- a-zh. 這位收藏家喜歡逛古董店尋找便宜好貨。
- b. If you explore the Internet, you'll probably find the item you're looking for at a good price.
- b-zh. 如果你上網搜尋,很可能會用不錯的價錢找到你要的商品。
- 衍生: exploration 探索;explorer 探險家
- 同義: investigate、look into

## 8. item (n.) — 項目、商品

- a. The grocery store has a special checkout line for people who are purchasing fewer than ten items.
- a-zh. 這家雜貨店為購買少於十件商品的顧客設有專用結帳通道。
- b. Do you think I can get all these items into one bag?
- b-zh. 你覺得我能把這些東西全部裝進一個袋子嗎?
- 用法: 可數名詞,所以用 fewer than ten items,不用 less
- 搭配: an item on the list 清單上的項目;news item 一則新聞

## 9. mandatory (adj.) — 強制的、必須的

- a. The jewelry store has a mandatory policy of showing customers only one item at a time.
- a-zh. 這家珠寶店有項強制規定,一次只向顧客展示一件商品。
- b. There is a mandatory limit of nine items for use of this checkout line.
- b-zh. 這條結帳通道有九件商品的強制上限。
- 同義: required、obligatory
- 反義: optional 選擇性的;voluntary 自願的

## 10. merchandise (n.) — 商品

- a. I am very impressed with the quality of the merchandise on this website.
- a-zh. 這個網站上商品的品質讓我印象深刻。
- b. Helen made sure that the store had a wide variety of merchandise before buying a gift certificate.
- b-zh. 海倫在購買禮券前,先確認這家店有各式各樣的商品。
- 注意: 不可數名詞,不加 s
- 衍生: merchant 商人;merchandising 商品行銷
- 同義: goods

## 11. strictly (adv.) — 嚴格地

- a. Our store strictly enforces its return policy.
- a-zh. 我們店嚴格執行退貨政策。
- b. Their high turnover rate is no surprise, considering how strictly the manager deals with them.
- b-zh. 考慮到經理對待他們有多嚴格,他們的高離職率並不令人意外。
- 搭配: strictly speaking 嚴格說來;strictly forbidden 嚴禁
- 衍生: strict 嚴格的;strictness 嚴格

## 12. trend (n.) — 時尚、潮流

- a. The clothing store tries to stay on top of all the new trends.
- a-zh. 這家服飾店努力跟上所有新的潮流。
- b. Mioshi followed market trends closely before she bought a clothing franchise.
- b-zh. 米歐西在買下一家服飾加盟店之前,密切關注了市場趨勢。
- 搭配: follow / set a trend 追隨或引領潮流;market trend 市場趨勢
- 衍生: trendy 流行的;trendsetter 潮流引領者
